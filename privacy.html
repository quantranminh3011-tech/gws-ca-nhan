Privacy policy

gws-ca-nhan is a private tool used by its owner only, with the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool can access that account's Google Drive, Gmail, Sheets, Docs, Calendar and Tasks data through Google APIs.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests. Results are handled on the owner's computer and may be processed by the AI assistant the owner chooses to run the tool with.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. The tool has no server or database of its own. Data is not sold, and is not used for advertising.

Other people

The tool does not offer accounts to anyone else and does not collect data from visitors to this page.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
